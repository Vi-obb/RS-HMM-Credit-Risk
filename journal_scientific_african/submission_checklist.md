# Scientific African submission-fit checklist

Verified against the live Elsevier/ScienceDirect Guide for Authors on 27 July 2026. Do not replace these values with the repository's earlier checklist without rechecking the official page.

Official sources:

- Guide for Authors: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/scientific-african/publish/guide-for-authors>
- Aims and scope: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/scientific-african>
- Open-access options: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/scientific-african/publish/open-access-options>
- Submission portal: <https://submit.elsevier.com/SCIAF>

## Research-paper limits

- [x] Standalone original research paper.
- [x] No more than 7,500 words from Introduction through Conclusion, excluding references.
- [x] No more than six tables and figures combined: this draft contains four tables and no figures.
- [x] No more than 40 references.
- [x] Abstract no more than 250 words, factual, standalone, and without references.
- [x] Between one and seven English keywords: this draft contains six.
- [x] Clearly numbered sections and subsections; abstract is unnumbered.
- [x] Consistent reference format; every cited item must appear in the list and vice versa.

## Review and manuscript files

- [x] Single-anonymized review permits author details in the manuscript.
- [x] Editable LaTeX source and compiled PDF prepared.
- [x] Tables are editable text, numbered, captioned, and cited.
- [x] Highlights supplied as a separate editable file. They are encouraged, not mandatory: 3–5 bullets, each no more than 85 characters including spaces.
- [ ] Graphical abstract is encouraged, not mandatory. None is supplied in this package.
- [ ] If a graphical abstract is later supplied, use a separate TIFF/EPS/PDF/MS Office file, at least 531 by 1328 pixels (height by width) or proportional, readable at 5 by 13 cm.
- [ ] If figures are later added, upload each separately at the official resolution and include captions in the manuscript.

## Declarations and metadata

- [ ] Confirm final author list and order before submission; the guide generally disallows authorship changes after submission and disallows them after acceptance.
- [ ] Designate one corresponding author and provide email, full postal address, and telephone.
- [ ] Complete Elsevier's declarations tool and upload the resulting `.doc`/`.docx` competing-interest declaration.
- [ ] Confirm and disclose all funding and sponsor roles, or approve the no-specific-funding statement.
- [x] Include the required generative-AI preparation disclosure before the references.
- [x] Provide a data/code availability statement; add a permanent public archive identifier if created.
- [ ] Obtain the institution's ethics determination for secondary use of de-identified loan data and insert committee/date/reference or an explanation of exemption.
- [ ] Confirm CRediT roles after authorship is final. CRediT is encouraged by the guide.
- [ ] Complete acknowledgements or state “None.”
- [ ] Confirm all authors approve submission and that the article is not under consideration elsewhere.
- [ ] Confirm any thesis repository status. The official submission declaration permits work previously available as an academic thesis.

## Journal fit and unavoidable risk

- [x] Economics and Business and Mathematics are within the journal's stated categories.
- [x] Title, abstract, introduction, discussion, conclusion, and cover letter explicitly identify the United States empirical setting.
- [x] No claim of African empirical validation or universal regime-aware superiority.
- [x] No citation to the unreviewed Brookfield preprint.
- [ ] Editorial scope remains uncertain because the empirical portfolio is not African. The defensible fit is methodological research-capacity value; direct African policy or borrower claims are out of scope.
- [ ] Confirm willingness and eligibility for the current open-access APC shown by the journal at submission; the official open-access page should be rechecked immediately before external submission.

## Final portal checks

- [ ] Spell and grammar check approved by all authors.
- [ ] Cross-check all text citations against the reference list.
- [ ] Confirm that all uploaded files, tables, captions, supplementary files, and declarations open correctly.
- [ ] Confirm permissions for any third-party copyrighted content; this draft uses no reproduced third-party figure.
- [ ] Recheck the live Guide for Authors on the day of submission.
