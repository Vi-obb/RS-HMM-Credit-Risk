# Cover letter draft — Scientific African

Dear Editors,

Please consider the research paper “A Regime-Aware Model for Loan-Level Probability of Default” for the Economics and Business or Mathematics category of *Scientific African*.

The manuscript evaluates an interpretable regime-aware mortgage probability-of-default model under a causal rolling-origin protocol. Its contribution is methodological and deliberately cautious: an initially favourable terminal result does not persist across forecast origins, borrower-only logistic regression is stronger on pooled causal proper scores, discrimination, and monthly count calibration, and nearly saturated hidden-state probabilities limit hard-regime comparisons.

The empirical application uses United States Freddie Mac and FRED data. The manuscript does not claim African external validity. It instead offers an evaluation framework relevant to African researchers developing temporally robust credit-risk models and states explicitly that direct regional inference requires locally defined outcomes, macroeconomic indicators, and validation data. We recognise that the absence of an African empirical portfolio is a scope risk and invite editorial assessment of the manuscript's methodological fit.

The work has not been submitted elsewhere. All authors will approve the final submission and declarations. The manuscript is derived from an academic thesis, which the journal's current submission declaration permits, and has been rewritten as a standalone research article.
