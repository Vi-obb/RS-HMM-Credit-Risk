\chapter{Methodology}

\section{Data Sources}

The empirical workflow combines two public data sources:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Freddie Mac sample origination and servicing files for mortgage cohorts from 2015 through 2025
    \item FRED monthly macroeconomic data for inflation, the Federal Funds rate, and unemployment
\end{itemize}

The mortgage data provides the loan-level panel structure, while the macro data supplies the aggregate environment used for regime inference.

\section{Unit Of Analysis}

The unit of observation is one mortgage at one month. Each loan-month observation combines:

\begin{itemize}
    \item origination characteristics,
    \item allowed current performance indicators from servicing records,
    \item month-level macro variables, and
    \item a forward-looking default proxy label.
\end{itemize}

\section{Primary Label}

The primary label is a 12-month transition to 90+ days past due. For a loan-month indexed by $(i,t)$, the target equals one when the loan is not already 90+ DPD at month $t$ and reaches serious delinquency within the next 12 months. Observations without a full forward horizon are excluded from model training and evaluation.

\section{Macro Regime Inference}

Let $M_t$ denote the monthly macro vector containing inflation, the Federal Funds rate, and unemployment. A two-state Gaussian hidden Markov model is fit to the standardized macro series:

\[
    M_t \mid R_t = r \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_r, \Sigma_r),
\]

where $R_t \in \{1,2\}$ is the latent macro regime. The main downstream quantity is the filtered stress probability

\[
    p_{\text{stress}}(t) = \Pr(R_t = \text{stress} \mid M_{1:t}).
\]

Filtered rather than smoothed probabilities ensure that the downstream PD model does not depend on future macro information.

\section{Model Comparison}

The active empirical study will compare exactly three logistic specifications:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item borrower-only baseline
    \item macro-augmented baseline
    \item regime-aware logit with $p_{\text{stress}}(t)$ and borrower-feature interactions
\end{enumerate}

The regime-aware specification is

\[
    \Pr(Y_{i,t}^{(12)} = 1 \mid x_{i,t}, p_{\text{stress}}(t))
    =
    \sigma \left(
        \beta_0 + \beta^\top x_{i,t} + \gamma p_{\text{stress}}(t)
        + p_{\text{stress}}(t)\theta^\top x_{i,t}
    \right).
\]

This keeps the final model interpretable while allowing borrower risk sensitivities to vary with macro stress intensity.

\section{Evaluation Plan}

Evaluation is calibration-first. The primary performance tools are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Brier score
    \item calibration plots
    \item calibration by regime slice and time slice
\end{itemize}

Secondary metrics include ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and log loss. All comparisons use chronological splits only.

\section{Implementation Status}

The repository documents this methodology and keeps the legacy synthetic scaffold available only in the archive. The active project is the Freddie Mac ingestion and empirical modeling workflow.
